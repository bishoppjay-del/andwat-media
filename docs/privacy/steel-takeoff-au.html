Steel Takeoff AU: Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Applies to the "Steel Takeoff AU" Chrome extension published by Andwat Co (sole trader, Brisbane, Australia, ABN 77 173 994 186).

Summary

Your takeoffs stay in your browser. We don't run a server, don't collect analytics, and don't see what you enter. The only outside service the extension talks to is ExtensionPay (which uses Stripe), and only when you choose to buy Pro or log in to Pro.

What is stored, and where

  • In your browser (chrome.storage.local): your jobs and takeoff lines (marks, sections, lengths, quantities), prices and saw kerf you enter, Rev compare settings (rate, variation ref, project, client, drawing refs), and a cached "Pro: yes/no" status. This stays on your computer. Remove the extension to delete it.
  • Selected text: when you right-click selected text and choose "Add selection to Steel Takeoff", that text is passed to the side panel and briefly stored locally until it is read. The extension doesn't read web pages by itself and has no permission to access websites.
  • ExtensionPay licence key (chrome.storage.sync): once you start a purchase or log in, the ExtensionPay library stores an anonymous key and install date. Chrome may sync this through your Google account if you have sync turned on.

What is sent off your computer

  • Nothing while you use the free (Lite) features.
  • Payments: when you click "Unlock Pro" or "Already paid? Log in", the extension contacts extensionpay.com to create the anonymous key above and to check whether you have paid. Payment happens on ExtensionPay and Stripe pages, where you give your email and card details to them, not to us. The extension then receives only your paid status and plan. No takeoff data is ever sent. See ExtensionPay and the Stripe Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking, ads, or remote code.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • No use of data for anything other than the extension's single purpose (steel takeoffs).

Your choices

Delete a job or clear it inside the extension, or remove the extension to erase all local data. For questions about a payment, or to cancel a subscription, use the payment page opened from the extension (ExtensionPay lets you manage your subscription there), or contact us.
