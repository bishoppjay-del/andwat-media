QBCC variation form: how to document a variation before you start work (Queensland domestic building)

Last reviewed 26 November 2026 · Sources linked below · Written by Jay at Andwat Co, an Australian boilermaker/fabricator (Cert III Engineering, 12+ years in the trade). About

On a Queensland home job over $3,300, a verbal "yeah, just do it" isn't enough. The QBCC Act says the owner has to agree to the variation in writing before you start the extra work, and your variation document has to contain specific details. Here's what the law requires, where to find QBCC's own variation forms, and a free checklist to fill in.

The short answer

  • Applies to: regulated domestic building contracts in Queensland (domestic building work over $3,300), under Schedule 1B of the QBCC Act 1991.
  • Before you start: you must not start the variation work until the owner agrees to the variation in writing (Sch 1B s 40(5)). Maximum penalty 20 penalty units.
  • Give a copy: you must give the owner a copy of the variation in writing before the earlier of 5 business days after you agree it, or the variation work starting (s 40(2)). You can hand it over, post it, email it, or use the notice method in the contract.
  • Urgent work exception: it's a defence to the copy requirement if the work was urgent and it wasn't reasonably practicable to produce the written copy first (s 40(4)).
  • No payment in advance: you can't require payment of a variation price increase before the variation work starts (s 41(3)).
  • Contract still stands: failing to comply doesn't make the contract illegal, void or unenforceable (s 44), but it's an offence and it weakens your position in a dispute.

What the variation document must say

Schedule 1B s 41 and QBCC's contract changes page list the formal requirements. The document must:

  1. be readily legible
  2. describe the variation
  3. state the date of the request for the variation
  4. if it will delay the work, state your reasonable estimate of the delay
  5. state the change to the contract price, or how the change will be calculated (QBCC's example is a rate such as $50 per square metre)
  6. state when a price increase is to be paid, or when a decrease is to be credited

QBCC's guide for owners and contractors adds that the document should be dated and signed by the contractor (QBCC domestic building contracts guide).

Where to find the QBCC variation form

QBCC publishes variation forms with its contract templates. On the contract changes and variations page, QBCC says they're listed as Form 4 or Form 5 in the forms and schedules for each contract, depending on the contract type. If you use an industry association contract (HIA, Master Builders), use the variation form that comes with that contract.

If the variation delays the job, an extension of time is a separate written claim under Sch 1B s 42, with its own 10 business day limit. See the QBCC extension of time guide, and keep the site diary going.

Commercial jobs and subcontracts are different

Schedule 1B only covers domestic building contracts. On commercial building contracts and subcontracts, the variation rules come from your contract. The QBCC Act s 67I does help if you're given a direction verbally: unless the contract says otherwise, you can ask for it in writing and you don't have to comply until you get it, and the contracting party commits an offence if it isn't put in writing within 3 business days. "Direction" includes an instruction, approval or request (QBCC Act s 67I). Then claim the approved variation as its own line in your progress claim.

FAQ

Does a construction variation have to be in writing in Queensland?

For a regulated domestic building contract (home building work over $3,300), yes. Schedule 1B of the QBCC Act says the contractor must not start the variation work until the owner agrees in writing, and must give the owner a written copy of the variation within 5 business days of agreeing it or before the work starts, whichever is first. Commercial contracts and subcontracts follow their own contract terms.

Where do I get a QBCC variation form?

QBCC publishes variation forms with its domestic contract templates. Its contract changes and variations page says they're listed as Form 4 or Form 5 among each contract's forms and schedules, depending on the contract type.

What must a QBCC variation document include?

It must be readily legible, describe the variation, state the date it was requested, give your reasonable estimate of any delay, state the change to the contract price or how it's calculated, and say when an increase is to be paid or a decrease credited.

Can I ask for payment for a variation upfront?

No. On a regulated domestic contract, any increase in the contract price from a variation can't be required to be paid before the variation work starts.
