The problem

Most small fab shops still keep weld tests, White Cards, first aid, forklift tickets and the rest in a mix of folders, gloveboxes and message threads. Nothing tells you a ticket is about to expire. The first warning is a builder, a site induction or an auditor asking for paperwork you can’t put your hand on.

What the tracker does

  • Welder register. Add each welder and their tickets — weld tests, White Card, first aid, forklift, high-risk work licences, or any other ticket you need to track — with the expiry date and a photo or PDF of the certificate.
  • Expiry reminders. Email alerts 60, 30 and 7 days before a ticket runs out, plus a weekly summary for the boss.
  • Audit export. One click gives a PDF or CSV of every welder’s current tickets, ready for a builder, auditor or main contractor.

Built for Australian fab shops with about 5–30 welders. You stay in charge of the records; the tracker just keeps them in one place and flags what’s due.

Questions

What tickets can I track?

Whatever your crew actually carries. Typical ones are weld tests, White Cards, first aid, forklift and high-risk work licences. You can add other tickets and certificates too. This is a reminder and register tool — it doesn’t issue tickets or replace a qualified person’s check.

Is my data safe?

For this early access list we only ask for your email and, if you want, a shop name and crew size. We’ll use that to invite you when the tracker is ready, and we won’t sell the list.

When the app itself is built, each shop will have its own login. Certificate photos and PDFs you upload will sit in your shop’s account so you can pull them up for an audit. We will update the privacy policy before any of that goes live.
