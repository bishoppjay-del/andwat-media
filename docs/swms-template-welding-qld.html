How to fill a welding SWMS template for Queensland sites: the high-risk boxes, method steps, controls that get accepted, and a free one-page sample you can print.

Queensland principal contractors keep asking for a SWMS before welding starts. The law sits in the Work Health and Safety Regulation 2011 (Qld), which mirrors the national high-risk construction work rules. Our HRCW guide and mobile welder SWMS guide cover the why. This page is the template how-to: what to type into each box so the site accepts it.

The short answer

  • A welding SWMS is required when the work is one of the 18 high-risk construction work types. WorkSafe Queensland publishes SWMS guidance aligned with Safe Work Australia.
  • Fill in the actual site: height, plant, tanks, traffic, electrical. Blank "N/A" rows get knocked back.
  • List the sequence of work, the hazards for each step, and the controls you will actually use on that job.
  • Give a copy to the principal contractor before work starts, brief the crew, and keep it available on site.
  • Start with our free one-page welding SWMS sample (PDF), then expand it for the full job.

Template fields that matter on a QLD welding job

  • Business / PCBU name, ABN, and who prepared and approved the SWMS
  • Exact high-risk construction work types that apply (tick only what is real)
  • Job address, date / duration, and who the principal contractor is
  • Step-by-step method (arrive → isolate → weld → fire watch → pack up)
  • Hazards and controls per step, with a named person responsible
  • PPE, plant, and permits (hot work, confined space, EWP)
  • How the crew is briefed and how the SWMS is reviewed if the job changes
  • Names and signatures of workers who will do the work

Worked example – ute welder on a Brisbane mezzanine

HRCW ticks: fall more than 2 m (scissor lift), powered mobile plant nearby (forklift feeding steel), near energised services (temporary board on the slab).

Key controls written on the SWMS: scissor lift ticket + harness rule per site; exclusion zone and spotter when the forklift moves; RCD on the welder lead; fire blankets and a named fire watch under the site's hot work permit; SWMS dated for this address and handed to the site manager before the first arc.

Printable snippet – minimum welding SWMS checklist

  • HRCW types named (not just "welding")
  • Site address and date match this job
  • Hot work / confined space / EWP permits cross-referenced
  • Fire extinguisher and fire watch named
  • Crew signed on
  • Principal contractor has a copy

Quick answers

Does Queensland require a different SWMS form?

No special state form is mandated. The content must meet the WHS Regulation requirements for high-risk construction work. WorkSafe Queensland accepts SWMS that cover the activity, hazards and controls for that site.

Can I use a national welding SWMS template in QLD?

Yes as a starting point, but you must review it against the Queensland site hazards and the principal contractor's rules before work starts.

Is a free one-page SWMS enough?

It is a starter for a simple job. Complex height, tank or plant work needs a fuller document. Sites often reject one-pagers that skip HRCW types or crew signatures.
