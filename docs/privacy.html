Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Short version: this site doesn't ask for or collect your personal information, and it doesn't set cookies or run analytics or ads. The free calculators keep your figures in your own browser.

Who we are

Andwat Trade Guides is run by Andwat Co, a sole trader in Queensland, Australia (ABN 77 173 994 186). Contact us via the contact page.

What this site collects

  • No forms, accounts or sign-ups. The site is static pages. The only scripts are the two free calculators, which run in your browser and make no network requests.
  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • Calculator figures stay on your device. The charge-out rate calculator and the GST & BAS calculator save the figures you type in your browser's local storage, so they're there next time you visit. They're never sent to us or anyone else. To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts are installed, and no advertising is shown.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit any website, your browser sends technical information such as your IP address and browser type. GitHub may log this to run and secure its service. We don't have access to those logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

Guides link to government and industry websites and to the Andwat Co store on Payhip. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Some pages contain Amazon affiliate links, and Amazon may set cookies when you click them. If you buy a template or use the contact form, that happens on Payhip and is covered by Payhip's privacy policy.

If this changes

We may add privacy-friendly analytics or advertising (for example Google AdSense) in future. If we do, this page will be updated before they go live. It will say what is collected, which third parties set cookies, what they're used for (for example, Google and its partners using cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites), and how to opt out (for example through Google's Ads Settings).

Questions or complaints

Contact us via the contact page. You can also find information about privacy rights in Australia from the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
