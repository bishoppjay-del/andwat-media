Free tools for Australian tradies

Last reviewed 25 November 2026 · Free · No sign-up

Interactive calculators and printable checklists that run in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us. Each tool pairs with a plain-English guide and, where it helps, a matching Andwat Co template at list price.

Interactive calculators and checklists

  • Overdue invoice interest calculator
    Simple interest from the QLD BIF Act / court rate (10.35% p.a. for July–December 2026), the s67P penalty rate, or your own agreed rate.
  • Galvanizing vent and drain hole lookup
    GAA 2026 minimum hole sizes for common SHS, RHS and CHS, plus the 25% rule for a custom size and the overlap table.
  • Licence, ticket and insurance expiry checker
    Days to expiry, with the Queensland HRWL “lodge on time / 12-month renewal” rules when you need them.
  • Printable plant pre-start checklist
    Tick OK / FAULT / N/A for a daily walk-around, sign off, and print an A4 copy to keep with the machine.
  • Tradie Charge-Out Rate Calculator
    Hourly and day rate that covers your wage, super, overheads and unbillable hours, plus profit, with or without GST.
  • GST & BAS Quick Calculator
    Add or remove 10% GST, estimate G1, 1A and 1B for a Simpler BAS, and check the $75,000 GST registration threshold (this is the $75k checker — we did not duplicate it).
  • Steel Takeoff Pro
    AU steel takeoff, mass schedule and cut-list nesting in the browser. Free for 15 members; Pro from A$39/month.

Other free resources

  • Free steel cut list checklist
    Printable A4 checklist for nesting pieces into stock lengths, with kerf and end trim, before you buy bars.
  • Steel cut list worked example
    A plain-English walk-through of nesting RHS/SHS into 6, 9 or 12 m stock (guide, not a live optimiser).
  • Free welding SWMS sample
    A sample safe work method statement you can read before buying a full pack.
  • Car & 4WD tools
    Jump starters, OBD2 scanners and tyre inflators, with Amazon AU links. We may earn a commission as an Amazon Associate.
  • Welder Ticket Tracker early access list
    Join the waitlist if you want to be told when the welder ticket tracker is ready. Free to join, no obligation.

Guides behind these tools include interest on overdue invoices, galvanizing vent holes, QLD HRWL renewal, plant pre-starts, GST registration and quoting with GST.
