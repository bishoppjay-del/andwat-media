Simpler BAS: what goes in G1, 1A and 1B

Last reviewed 25 October 2026 · Sources linked below · Written by Jay at Andwat Co, an Australian boilermaker/fabricator (Cert III Engineering, 12+ years in the trade). About

Plain-English guide to the three GST boxes on a Simpler BAS – G1 Total sales, 1A GST on sales and 1B GST on purchases – with a worked example and ATO sources.

If your business is registered for GST and your GST turnover is under $10 million, the ATO's default way to report GST is Simpler BAS. For GST you only fill in three labels each period: G1, 1A and 1B. You don't need a GST calculation worksheet, and labels like G2, G3, G10 and G11 belong to the full reporting method, not Simpler BAS.

G1 – Total sales

G1 is all your sales for the period. That includes taxable sales (with GST), GST-free sales and input-taxed sales. You can report G1 either GST-inclusive or GST-exclusive, but you must tick which one you used.

  • The ATO's guide puts sales of goods and services, sales of business assets such as vehicles and machinery, GST-free sales and input-taxed sales at G1.

1A – GST on sales

1A is the GST included in your taxable sales. If you charged 10% on top of a GST-exclusive price, it's that 10%. If you only have the GST-inclusive total, the GST is 1/11 of the total.

1B – GST on purchases

1B is the GST included in the price of things you bought for your GST-registered business. These are your GST credits. You don't report total purchases on a Simpler BAS, only the GST in them.

  • You need a valid tax invoice to claim the credit on a purchase over $82.50 (GST inclusive).
  • No credit if the supplier isn't registered for GST, so there's no GST in the price. The same goes for GST-free items, bank fees, stamp duty, wages and super.
  • If you use something for both business and private purposes, such as a phone or a car, only claim the business share.

Worked example (made-up figures)

This quarter a sole trader invoiced $22,000 including GST, all taxable. They also bought $5,500 of materials and fuel including GST, all for the business, with tax invoices.

  • G1 Total sales (GST-inclusive): $22,000
  • 1A GST on sales: $22,000 ÷ 11 = $2,000
  • 1B GST on purchases: $5,500 ÷ 11 = $500
  • Net GST to pay: $2,000 − $500 = $1,500

Common mistakes

  • Claiming GST credits on purchases from suppliers who aren't registered for GST.
  • Claiming the full GST on a phone bill or car cost that is partly private.
  • Leaving GST-free sales out of G1. They still go in G1, just with no GST at 1A.
  • Not keeping tax invoices for purchases over $82.50.
