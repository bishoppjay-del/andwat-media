Overdue invoice interest calculator (AU / QLD)

Last reviewed 25 November 2026 · Free · No sign-up · Runs in your browser

Work out simple interest on a late invoice using the Queensland rates already sourced on our interest guide, or type your own agreed rate. Nothing you enter leaves this device.

In most of Australia you can only charge interest if it was agreed in your quote, contract or written terms before the work. For Queensland construction contracts, BIF Act s73 sets interest at the greater of your contract rate and the court money-order rate, which Queensland Courts list as 10.35% a year for 1 July–31 December 2026. Building contracts covered by QBCC Act s67P use the penalty rate instead: 10% a year plus the RBA 90-day bill rate (or a higher contract rate). The maths is the same in every case:

Interest = amount owing × annual rate × days late ÷ 365.
Example from the guide: $4,000 that is 30 days late at 10.35% a year → 4,000 × 0.1035 × 30 ÷ 365 = $34.03.

Interest is worked from the day after the due date to the day you are paid. This is general information, not legal or tax advice, and it does not decide whether you are allowed to charge interest on a particular invoice.

Privacy: the calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us or anyone else, and the page loads no trackers, analytics or third-party scripts. So your figures are still there next time, they're saved in this browser's local storage on your device only. To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings, or press Reset.
