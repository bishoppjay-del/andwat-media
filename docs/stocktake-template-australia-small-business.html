Stocktake Excel template for Australian small businesses: the count sheet columns, how to run the count, and closing variances

Do you even need a 30 June count?

Maybe not. Under the ATO's simplified trading stock rules, a small business that reasonably estimates its stock value changed by $5,000 or less over the year doesn't have to do a formal stocktake. If it changed by more, you count and value stock as close as possible to the end of the income year. The full rules, eligibility and the ATO's electrician example are in our stocktake and ATO trading stock rules guide. This page is the practical part: the spreadsheet, the count and the variances. A regular count is worth doing anyway, because it's how you find stock that's walking out of the van.

Free stocktake spreadsheet: the columns to set up

One row per item or variant (size, colour, length). These are the columns I'd put in Excel or Google Sheets. The grey ones are formulas.

ColumnWhat goes in it
LocationVan, shelf A3, back room. Count one location at a time.
SKU / item codeThe same code your POS, Shopify or invoice system uses.
Description and unitWrite the unit you count in: each, box of 100, metre, roll. Mixing cartons and singles is the most common counting error.
System quantityWhat your records say you should have at the count time.
Count 1First physical count.
Count 2 (recount)Only needed where count 1 doesn't match the system, ideally by a different person.
Final counted quantityThe agreed figure after the recount.
Variance (formula)Final counted minus system quantity. Negative means stock is missing.
Unit value ex GSTCost, market selling value or replacement value. Note which one.
Valuation methodCost / market selling / replacement, so you can explain it later.
Line value (formula)Final counted quantity × unit value.
Condition flagOK, damaged, obsolete, reserved for a customer. Damaged and obsolete stock may need a lower value; talk to your tax agent before you write it down.
Counted by and date/timeInitials plus the time, so you can match it to sales made during the count.
Variance noteWhat you checked and found (see below).

Add a total row for line value. That total is your closing stock figure, and it becomes next year's opening value.

How to run the count (small shop, van or workshop)

  1. Pick the time. The ATO says as close as possible to the end of the income year, which is 30 June for most businesses.
  2. Freeze or log movements. Best case, stop selling and receiving while you count. If you can't close, write down every item that goes in or out during the count so you can adjust: count + in − out.
  3. Print or export the sheet by location, with system quantities hidden from whoever does count 1 if you can, so they count what's there rather than confirm a number.
  4. Count in the unit on the sheet. Open part-boxes and count them, or record them as a separate line.
  5. Recount anything that doesn't match before you investigate further.
  6. Value and total. Fill in unit values and pick the method for each line.
  7. Adjust your system only after each variance has a note, then save a locked copy of the finished sheet with your tax records.

Closing variances properly

Check these in order before you write stock off: a sale or delivery made during the count, a delivery received but not entered, the wrong unit (carton vs each), stock in the wrong location, an item used on a job but not charged, and returns not processed. Write down what you found and the adjustment you made. A variance you can't explain yet stays open rather than being forced to zero.

How long to keep the sheet

Stocktake sheets support your tax return, so keep them with your other business records. See how long to keep business records in Australia (generally five years).

FAQ

Is there a free stocktake Excel template for Australian small businesses?

You can build one in a few minutes in Excel or Google Sheets using the columns on this page: one row per item, with system quantity, first count, recount, final count, a variance formula, unit value ex GST, valuation method, a line value formula and a variance note. Total the line values for your closing stock figure.

What columns should a stocktake spreadsheet have?

At minimum: location, item code, description and counting unit, system quantity, first count, recount, final counted quantity, variance (counted minus system), unit value ex GST, valuation method, line value, a condition flag, who counted and when, and a variance note. Total the line values to get your closing stock figure.

How do I value stock for the ATO?

The ATO lets you value each item at cost (including costs to get it to its current condition and location, such as freight), market selling value, or replacement value. You can use different methods for different items, and if you're entitled to GST credits you generally leave the GST out of the value.

Can I do a stocktake while the shop is still trading?

Yes, if you record every item that comes in or goes out during the count and adjust for it (count plus items received minus items sold or used). Otherwise sales made mid-count show up as false variances.
