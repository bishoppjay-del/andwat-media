What is on the free page

How to use it in 10 minutes

  1. Print two copies.
  2. Tick only the HRCW types that apply on this site.
  3. Rewrite any control that is not real for this job (heights, tanks, plant).
  4. Brief the crew and get signatures.
  5. Hand one copy to the principal contractor; keep one on the ute.

Quick answers

Is this free SWMS legally enough?

It depends on the job and the site. Many principal contractors accept a short job-specific SWMS; others want more detail. You must review it against actual hazards before work starts.

Can I remove the Andwat Co footer?

For your own site use, adapt the content into your business template. Do not resell the PDF.

Does the free page include a hot work permit?

No. Use a separate hot work permit when the site requires one.
