Licence, ticket and insurance expiry checker (AU / QLD)

Last reviewed 25 November 2026 · Free · No sign-up · Runs in your browser

Count the days until a ticket, licence or insurance policy expires. For a Queensland high risk work licence it also applies the “lodge on or before expiry” and 12-month renewal rules from Workplace Health and Safety Queensland.

A Queensland HRWL is valid for 5 years unless cancelled. Lodge the renewal on or before the expiry date and the licence stays valid until WHSQ decides. Lodge it after expiry (up to 12 months) and you can still renew, but you cannot do that work until the renewal is granted. More than 12 months after expiry, WHSQ will not accept a renewal unless exceptional circumstances apply — you redo training and assessment. See the HRWL renewal guide. For insurance and other tickets, this page only counts the days; it does not set a legal renewal deadline.

Privacy: the checker runs entirely in your browser. Names and dates you save stay in this browser's local storage on your device only. Nothing is sent to us or to any regulator. To remove them, press Remove on a saved item, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

The checker

“Today” is the date on this device. “Renew now” means 14 days or less; “due soon” means 60 days or less — practical reminders, not legal deadlines. Queensland HRWL rules are applied only when you pick that type.

Enter an expiry date and press Check expiry.
