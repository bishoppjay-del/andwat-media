Galvanizing vent and drain hole lookup (GAA chart, AU)

Last reviewed 25 November 2026 · Free · No sign-up · Runs in your browser

Look up the Galvanizers Association of Australia minimum vent and drain hole sizes for common SHS, RHS and CHS, or apply the 25% rule to a custom size. Figures come from the GAA 2026 chart already published on our galvanizing guide.

Completely sealed fabrications cannot be galvanized. Capped hollow sections need holes at both ends, off centre and diagonally opposite — a single hole in the middle of a cap is not enough. The chart figures are the GAA minimum at each capped end. Always confirm with your galvanizer, and read the placement rules on the vent and drain holes guide.

Worked example from the guide (50×50 SHS): one 18 mm hole, or two 13 mm holes, or four 10 mm holes, at each end. Round up to the next drill or plasma size you have, never down.
