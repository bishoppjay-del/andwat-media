Printable plant pre-start checklist

Last reviewed 25 November 2026 · Free · No sign-up · Runs in your browser · Print to A4

A one-page daily pre-start you can tick on screen and print: walk-around, fluids, brakes and e-stop, plus a safe-to-operate sign-off. The items come from our plant pre-start guide. Adapt them to the manufacturer’s list for that machine.

WHS laws require plant to be maintained and inspected so it stays safe. A daily pre-start at the start of each shift, for each machine, is the common way sites and hire companies meet that duty. Faults go in a defect register with a tag-out — do not “run it careful” until a competent person clears it. Keep the sheets; insurers and investigators ask for them after incidents. See the plant pre-start checklist guide for the full context.

Privacy: the checklist runs entirely in your browser. Figures and names you type are saved in this browser's local storage on your device only, and are never sent to us. Use Print checklist to keep a paper copy with the machine. This page does not store records for you once you clear the browser data.

The checklist

Plant and operator

Walk-around and function checks (tick OK / FAULT / N/A)

Walk-around: body damage, loose guards, missing pins
Tyres or tracks: pressure, cuts, tension
Fluids: engine oil, hydraulic oil, coolant, fuel – no puddles under the machine
Hydraulic hoses and rams: leaks, bulges, damaged fittings
Lights, horn, reverse / travel alarm
Seatbelt / restraint and operator seat condition
Brakes, steering, controls, attachments
ROPS / FOPS condition (where fitted)
Fire extinguisher present and in date
Load chart readable; e-stop works
